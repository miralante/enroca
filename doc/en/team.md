# Supporting someone with Enroca

Ask whether the person wants to play. Agree on one idea for today. A physical board
can sit beside the screen. Let the learner select the pieces.

1. Read or listen to one lesson. Ask the learner to identify the example piece.
2. Invite an explanation in their own words or through a move.
3. Open that lesson’s two exercises. Allow time; there is no limit.
4. Use a hint or return to the lesson when helpful. Avoid answering for the learner.
5. Try a game with fewer pieces. Discuss a decision rather than winning.

Useful prompts: “Whose turn is it?”, “Where can this piece go?” and “Is the king safe?”
Change the physical position to check whether the rule transfers to another example.
Repeat another day if the person wants to.

Read and Solved indicate activity, not mastery. Supported progress includes corrected
answers. Enroca stores no clinical data and creates no assessment reports. If you
record observations separately, follow your setting’s agreed procedures.

Settings include text size, contrast, piece names and language. Listen uses available
local voices only; if none exist, read together. On small screens, “Move using a list”
provides larger controls than individual squares.

Before stable publication, review words, symbols, pacing, hints, touch access and
independence with learners and supporters. Do not claim easy-read, WCAG certification
or effectiveness before that review.
