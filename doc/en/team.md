# Supporting someone with Enroca

Agree on one idea for today. A physical board can sit alongside the screen.

1. Open a topic and let the person observe or point to the example.
2. Select Next: the same journey asks for a concrete action.
3. Allow time to respond. Hints and returning to the example are available.
4. Offer a challenge using that piece or a match with few pieces.
5. Change the position on a physical board to observe understanding in another context.

The interface shows completed goals only. Internally progress distinguishes hints,
review and correction; it does not record errors, timing or attempts. These signals
are not a mastery assessment and do not generate reports.

Settings offers larger text, contrast, piece names and language. “Choose by name”
offers larger controls than squares. There is no narration; game sounds are optional.

Review words, symbols, pace, touch controls, screen-reader use and independence
with people and supporters. No certification or effectiveness is claimed.
