# Challenges

All 12 challenges are available from the start.

| Board | Goal | Pieces |
|---|---|---|
| 4 × 4 | Reach a flag | Rook, bishop, knight and pawn |
| 6 × 6 | Find paths and capture | Rook, bishop, knight and pawn |
| 8 × 8 | Protect the king | Move the king, block or capture the attacker |

Flags mark the goals. In the first two groups only your white piece moves;
the other pieces stay still. In the last group, your king must end outside the attack.

Select a piece, then its destination. “Choose by name” offers another control.
Hint marks a move from your current position. Undo takes back a move.
Repeat starts the challenge again. The piece example returns to the same challenge.

There is no clock or penalty. Only completed goals are saved; closing or reloading
restarts the challenge position. Completion does not establish mastery or transfer.
